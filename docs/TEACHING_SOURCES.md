# Teaching material notes

The bundled lessons are original introductory summaries, not scraped articles or
downloaded books. They cover selected concepts and do not constitute encyclopedic
coverage. The response-selection and composition mechanisms can use only the
representations and passages supplied to the engine.

Primary reference pages consulted for selected lessons:

- NASA, [Sun facts](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/): the Sun as a star, plasma,
  nuclear fusion, gravity, and its role in the solar system.
- US National Archives, [Declaration of Independence transcript](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript):
  the Declaration's date and historical claims about independence and rights.
- United Nations, [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights):
  adoption on December 10, 1948, and the rights described by the declaration.

Further reference starting points for extending or checking the pack:

- NASA [eclipse information](https://science.nasa.gov/eclipses/).
- [OpenStax Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e).
- [OpenStax World History Volume 2](https://openstax.org/details/books/world-history-volume-2).
- [World Health Organization nutrition topics](https://www.who.int/health-topics/nutrition).

Historical period boundaries and continent classifications sometimes differ by
convention. The relevant lessons qualify these where appropriate. Scientific and
medical summaries are educational background rather than individualized advice.
New training material should use clear definitions, relevant examples, and
corrections when better evidence becomes available.

The tests verify selected expected facts, relevant topic recall, formatting, and
retention. They do not independently certify every factual statement or establish
general reasoning ability.
