# Original micro-stories, one complete sentence per line.
# Sentence boundaries are isolated by the trainer's TrainText calls.
Mira puts a red book on the table.
She opens the book after lunch.
Mira reads a story about a boat.
She closes the book before dinner.
Leo looks outside and sees rain.
He takes an umbrella from the door.
Leo walks to the library with his umbrella.
He borrows a book about birds.
Nora fills a pot with soil.
She plants a seed in the pot.
Nora waters the seed each morning.
A small green shoot grows from the seed.
Sam has three apples in a basket.
He gives one apple to a friend.
Sam has two apples left in the basket.
His friend thanks him for the apple.
A blue bus stops near the school.
The children wait beside the bus stop.
The driver opens the bus door.
The children get on the bus.
Ava feeds her cat before breakfast.
The cat drinks water from a bowl.
After eating the cat sleeps on a chair.
Ava reads quietly beside the chair.
Omar finds a broken wooden toy.
He repairs the toy with glue.
Omar waits for the glue to dry.
The toy is ready to use again.
Rain falls during the morning.
The children play a game inside.
The rain stops in the afternoon.
The children go outside to play.
Ella writes a letter to her grandmother.
She puts the letter in an envelope.
Ella writes an address on the envelope.
Her grandmother receives the letter later.
