# Meaning and usage are taught through English examples and context.
what does write mean? ~ define write ~ explain the word write | To write is to produce language in a recorded form, using characters or other symbols. People write to explain ideas, tell stories, communicate instructions, record observations, or explore a question. A useful piece of writing is shaped by its purpose and intended audience.\n\nWriting about a factual topic requires relevant information as well as clear organization. An instruction to write does not supply the missing facts by itself. When knowledge is incomplete, a writer should distinguish supported information from speculation.
what is an essay? ~ define essay ~ explain the word essay | An essay is a piece of writing that develops a topic, explanation, or argument. It usually contains connected paragraphs rather than a disconnected list of facts. Its structure depends on its purpose, the available evidence, and the expected audience.\n\nA clear essay introduces the subject, develops its main points, and makes the relationships between those points understandable. Producing longer text is not enough on its own: the content should remain relevant and supported. Different essays can organize the same knowledge in different ways.
what does absorb mean? ~ define absorb ~ explain the word absorb | To absorb something is to take it in. A sponge can absorb water, and a material can absorb light or heat. The precise meaning depends on what is being taken in and how the process works.\n\nFor example, the sentence 'The towel absorbs the spilled water' describes liquid entering the towel. The word can also be used figuratively: absorbing information means taking time to understand or retain it.
what does evidence mean? ~ define evidence ~ explain the word evidence | Evidence is information that helps support or challenge a claim. Observations, measurements, documents, and reliable testimony can all be evidence, depending on the question. Good reasoning considers how the evidence was obtained and whether other explanations fit it.\n\nFor example, wet ground is evidence that water reached a surface, but it does not prove rain caused it. A sprinkler or a spill might explain the same observation.
what does cause mean? ~ define cause ~ explain the word cause | A cause is a factor that helps bring about an event or change. An effect is the resulting event or change. In the sentence 'Heating caused the ice to melt,' heating is the proposed cause and melting is the effect.\n\nEvents can have several contributing causes. The fact that two events happen together does not by itself show that one caused the other, so causal explanations need supporting evidence.
what does compare mean? ~ define compare ~ explain the word compare | To compare things is to examine how they are similar and how they differ. A useful comparison identifies a purpose and relevant features rather than assuming two things are identical because they share one characteristic.\n\nFor example, comparing a bicycle and a bus might involve cost, speed, passenger capacity, or environmental effects. Which comparison is useful depends on whether someone is planning a journey, studying transport, or asking a different question.
what does contrast mean? ~ define contrast ~ explain the word contrast | To contrast things is to focus on their differences. The word can also describe a difference itself, such as a strong contrast between light and dark areas in a picture.\n\nFor example, one might contrast a liquid with a solid by examining how each responds to a container. Contrasting them does not require ignoring their similarities; both are forms of matter and can share other properties.
what does explain mean? ~ define explain ~ explain the word explain | To explain something is to make it easier to understand by describing its meaning, causes, structure, or operation. A useful explanation connects the parts of an idea and gives enough context for the listener's question.\n\nFor example, explaining why a plant needs light involves more than repeating that it needs light. It connects the observation to photosynthesis and the use of light energy. An explanation should also acknowledge limits in the available evidence.
what does infer mean? ~ define infer ~ explain the word infer | To infer something is to draw a conclusion from evidence and reasoning rather than receiving it as a direct statement. An inference can be reasonable without being certain. Its strength depends on the evidence and the available alternatives.\n\nFor example, seeing someone enter carrying a wet umbrella may support the inference that it is raining. The person might instead have just cleaned the umbrella, so more information could change the conclusion.
what does observe mean? ~ define observe ~ explain the word observe | To observe is to notice or measure something carefully. Observations may be made with ordinary senses or with instruments such as thermometers, cameras, and microscopes. Recording conditions helps other people understand what was observed.\n\nIt is useful to distinguish an observation from an interpretation. 'The liquid changed color' is an observation. A claim about the chemical reaction that caused the change is an explanation that may need additional tests.
what does predict mean? ~ define predict ~ explain the word predict | To predict is to state what is expected to happen before the outcome is known. A prediction may come from a scientific model, previous experience, a statistical pattern, or a guess. These sources do not all provide the same level of support.\n\nTesting a prediction means comparing it with what actually happens. A failed prediction can reveal a mistaken assumption or a limitation in a model. Predictions are especially useful when their meaning and conditions are specified clearly.
what does estimate mean? ~ define estimate ~ explain the word estimate | An estimate is an approximate value or judgment based on the information available. Estimation is useful when exact measurement is unnecessary, impractical, or not yet possible. It should be presented with an appropriate level of uncertainty.\n\nFor example, someone can estimate a journey's duration from distance and typical travel speed. Traffic or delays may change the actual result. Reporting more decimal places does not make an uncertain estimate more accurate.
what does accurate mean? ~ define accurate ~ explain the word accurate | Accurate means close to the truth or to an accepted reference value. In measurement, accuracy concerns how close a result is to the quantity being measured. Precision concerns how closely repeated results agree with one another.\n\nA measuring instrument can produce very similar readings that are all shifted away from the true value. Those readings may be precise but inaccurate. Checking the instrument against a suitable reference can help identify the problem.
what does uncertain mean? ~ define uncertain ~ explain the word uncertain | Uncertain means not fully known, established, or predictable from the available information. Uncertainty can arise because evidence is incomplete, measurements have limits, a word has several meanings, or different explanations remain possible.\n\nExpressing uncertainty helps distinguish knowledge from a guess. Saying 'I do not know yet' can be more informative than giving a confident but unsupported answer. A useful next step is to identify what evidence or explanation would resolve the question.
what does learn mean? ~ define learn ~ explain the word learn | To learn is to acquire or improve knowledge, skills, or understanding through experience, study, feedback, or practice. Learning often involves connecting new information with something already known and checking whether it can be used successfully.\n\nIn this engine, teaching words adds or reuses word neurons, strengthens connections, and stores context-specific dialogue examples. Those mechanisms support specific learned behavior; their usefulness should be checked with new questions rather than assumed from training alone.
what does remember mean? ~ define remember ~ explain the word remember | To remember is to retain or bring back information from past experience or learning. Human memory involves several processes and can be incomplete or mistaken. Remembering a statement does not establish that the statement was true.\n\nFor this engine, persistent recall means loading learned representations and dialogue contexts from its brain file. A saved answer should still be tested for relevance to the current question. Repetition can reinforce a memory without improving its accuracy.
what does a subject do in a sentence? ~ explain grammatical subjects ~ explain subjects in english | In many English sentences, the subject identifies what the sentence is about and often the participant performing an action. In 'The dog chased the cat,' the dog is the subject and the cat is the object of the verb chased.\n\nWord order matters because exchanging the two nouns changes who performs the action. Subjects are not always active agents: in 'The cat was chased by the dog,' the cat is the grammatical subject even though the dog performs the chasing.
what do pronouns refer to? ~ explain pronoun reference ~ explain pronouns in context | Pronouns can refer to people, things, or ideas without repeating a full noun phrase. Words such as she, he, it, they, and we depend on the surrounding context for their reference.\n\nIn 'Mira opened her book because she wanted to read,' the word she normally refers to Mira. In a more complicated sentence, the reference may be ambiguous. When the context does not identify the intended person or thing, asking for clarification is better than assuming.

what does context mean? ~ define context ~ explain the word context | Context is the surrounding information that helps determine how a word, statement, or event should be understood. It can include earlier sentences, the topic of a conversation, the situation, and what the participants already know.\n\nFor example, bank can refer to a financial institution or to the side of a river. Nearby words and the speaker's purpose help distinguish those meanings. Knowing a word's spelling alone is not enough to resolve every use of it.
