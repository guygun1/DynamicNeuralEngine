# Grammar, vocabulary, meaning, and simple usage.
what is a noun? | a noun names a person place thing or idea.
give an example of a noun | book is a noun.
what is a verb? | a verb describes an action or a state.
give an example of a verb | run is a verb.
what is an adjective? | an adjective describes a noun.
give an example of an adjective | blue is an adjective.
what is an adverb? | an adverb can describe how an action happens.
give an example of an adverb | slowly is an adverb.
what is a pronoun? | a pronoun can take the place of a noun.
give an example of a pronoun | she is a pronoun.
what is a sentence? | a sentence expresses a complete thought.
what starts a written sentence? | a written sentence usually starts with a capital letter.
what ends a question? | a question mark ends a written question.
what ends a statement? | a period usually ends a written statement.
what does a comma do? | a comma separates parts of a sentence.
