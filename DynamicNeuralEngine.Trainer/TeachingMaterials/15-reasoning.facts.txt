# Explicit premises, not prewritten answers to the inference questions.
# Controlled English currently supports is-a, has, needs, and is-in statements.
A robin is a bird.
A canary is a bird.
A penguin is a bird.
A bird is an animal.
A cat is a mammal.
A dog is a mammal.
A mammal is an animal.
An animal is a living organism.
A plant is a living organism.
A tree is a plant.
A living organism needs energy.
A plant needs water.
A bird has wings.
A mammal has a backbone.
A solar eclipse is an astronomical event.
An astronomical event is a natural phenomenon.
The French Revolution is a revolution.
A revolution is a political change.
A political change is a historical event.
Paris is in France.
France is in Europe.
